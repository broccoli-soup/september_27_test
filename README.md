# september_27_test
September 27 test project

We would depart for England aboard a steamship the next morning. Upon arrival, I settled into one of my father’s estates, Thornfield. This manor house lived up to his dreary descriptions for its ivy-clad three-story frame sat beneath a perpetual layer of thick fog. He hired a French-speaking nurse, Sophie, to care for me. Though I cannot intimately remember the details of her character, I can, with great affection, recall moments we spent playing in Thornfield’s front yard and sewing together in the drawing room. However, as I grew older, my developmental needs slowly became incompatible with Sophie’s talents. He dismissed Sophie and hired a governess by the name of Miss Eyre, whom he hoped would guide my intellect and character in his place. However, I do not believe that any individual other than her and Sophie held me in genuine positive regard.
