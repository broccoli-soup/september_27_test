September 27 test project

There was no possibility of taking a walk that day. We had been wandering, indeed, through the leafless trees by the Seine. I was glad of it; I never liked long walks, especially on chilly winter afternoons. At dusk, my father, whom you may know as Mr. Rochester, gently swayed his rocking chair while I stood on a nearby fur rug. His countenance was illuminated by the fireplace’s hearty orange flame. He smoked a long pinewood cigar, staring. His eyes, devoid of all warmth and compassion, fixed themselves upon faraway clouds and the raindrops pattering against the veranda.
